Frequently asked questions

Is TaskBuddies free?

It is free to download and the core features are free. Tasks, expense logging, debt tracking and chat are open to everyone.

How do I add my family or team?

Create a family or team and invite members by email. Anyone who taps the invite link joins straight in the app.

What is the difference between Home and Business mode?

Home mode is for chores, shared expenses and the family budget. Business mode is for task approval, expense approval, project budgets and reports.

Does the other person have to see the debt record?

Yes, debt records are two-sided. The other person confirms or disputes it, so both sides see the same number.

Is my data safe?

Your data travels over an encrypted connection and is only visible to you and the people you invite. You can delete your account anytime.
